Birbilgibirinsan Uploader

English: Birbilgibirinsan Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Birbilgibirinsan" to upload and schedule the channel's own short educational science videos, read the channel's own video statistics, and post comments on the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Türkçe: Birbilgibirinsan Uploader, yalnızca "Birbilgibirinsan" YouTube kanalının sahibinin kullandığı özel bir araçtır. Kanalın kendi kısa bilim videolarını yükler ve zamanlar, kanalın kendi istatistiklerini okur ve kanalın kendi videolarına yorum yazar. Başka kullanıcısı yoktur.

Google API usage

  • YouTube Data API: upload, schedule, update and delete the owner's own videos; post comments on them.
  • YouTube Analytics API: read the owner's own channel statistics.

Birbilgibirinsan Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
