Privacy Policy — Birbilgibirinsan Uploader

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What this app is

Birbilgibirinsan Uploader is a private automation tool operated solely by the owner of the YouTube channel "Birbilgibirinsan" for managing that channel. It has no other users and does not collect data from the public.

Data accessed

  • The owner's YouTube channel: uploading, scheduling, updating and deleting the owner's own videos, and posting comments on them.
  • The owner's own video statistics (views, likes, comments, average view percentage) via YouTube Analytics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to improve future videos based on the channel's own statistics.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted in the owner's private GitHub repository. Statistics are stored in the same private repository.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Türkçe özet

Bu araç yalnızca kanal sahibinin kendi YouTube kanalını yönetmek için kullanılır. Erişilen veriler (kanalın kendi videoları ve istatistikleri) sadece video yayınlamak ve gelecekteki videoları iyileştirmek için kullanılır, üçüncü kişilerle paylaşılmaz. İzin anahtarı şifreli olarak sahibin özel deposunda saklanır. Erişim myaccount.google.com/permissions adresinden her an kaldırılabilir.
